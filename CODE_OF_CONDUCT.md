# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

MQL5 Engineering is an open-source engineering project. We want participation to be professional, technically rigorous, welcoming, and safe for contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Participants are expected to:

- discuss ideas, code, evidence, and trade-offs rather than attacking people;
- give technical criticism clearly and respectfully;
- distinguish facts, hypotheses, opinions, and environment-dependent behavior;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, and deliberate disruption;
- respect contributor privacy and do not publish private information without permission;
- avoid using project spaces to promote scams, misleading trading claims, or guaranteed-profit claims.

## Engineering discussions

Disagreement is normal and useful. When technical conclusions differ, prefer reproducible evidence, project requirements, and authoritative MQL5 documentation over status, seniority, or rhetoric.

A strong review may be adversarial toward assumptions and code paths, but it must remain respectful toward contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct, doxxing, spam, malicious disruption, or repeated bad-faith interaction.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and participation that violate this policy. Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

For sensitive conduct concerns, contact the maintainer privately through the GitHub profile associated with this repository rather than opening a public issue.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, releases, and other project-managed community spaces.
