# Debugging discipline

1. Define expected/observed behavior.
2. Build a tight safe feedback loop.
3. Reproduce and minimize.
4. Generate 3-5 falsifiable hypotheses for complex bugs.
5. Instrument only boundaries that distinguish them.
6. Establish root cause.
7. Propose smallest safe fix.
8. Obtain approval.
9. Re-run reproduction and regressions.
10. Remove instrumentation.
